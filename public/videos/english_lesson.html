What is an Essay?

An essay is a piece of writing that presents an argument or discusses a topic.

Key Elements:

  • Introduction
  • Body paragraphs
  • Conclusion

Essay Structure

Introduction

Hook reader, state thesis, outline main points

Body

3-5 paragraphs, each with topic sentence and supporting details

Conclusion

Restate thesis, summarize main points, final thought

Types of Essays

  • Descriptive - paints a picture with words
  • Narrative - tells a story
  • Expository - explains or informs
  • Persuasive - convinces reader

Writing Tips

  • Plan before writing
  • Use clear topic sentences
  • Provide specific examples
  • Use transition words
  • Proofread carefully
