🔨 Woodwork Project Tutorial

Woodwork Lesson for GCE O/L Students

Teacher: Mr. James Taylor | Duration: 48:25

Lesson Objectives

  • Learn woodwork safety
  • Master basic tools
  • Understand wood types
  • Complete a simple project

Safety First

• Wear safety goggles

• Use push sticks

• Keep work area clean

• Follow tool instructions

Basic Tools

Hammer - Driving nails

Saw - Cutting wood

Chisel - Shaping wood

Plane - Smoothing surfaces

Wood Types

Hardwood - Oak, mahogany

Softwood - Pine, cedar

Plywood - Layered wood

MDF - Engineered wood

Simple Project: Box

1. Measure and cut pieces

2. Join edges with glue

3. Reinforce with nails

4. Sand and finish
