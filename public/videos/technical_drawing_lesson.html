📐 Technical Drawing Demo

Technical Drawing Lesson for GCE O/L Students

Teacher: Eng. Michael Wilson | Duration: 55:10

Lesson Objectives

  • Understand technical drawing basics
  • Learn drawing instruments
  • Master line types
  • Practice geometric constructions

Drawing Instruments

T-Square - Horizontal lines

Set Square - Vertical/angled lines

Compass - Circles and arcs

Protractor - Angle measurement

Line Types

Object lines - Thick, solid

Hidden lines - Dashed

Center lines - Alternating long/short dashes

Dimension lines - Thin with arrows

Basic Geometric Constructions

• Bisecting lines

• Drawing perpendicular lines

• Constructing angles

• Drawing circles and arcs
