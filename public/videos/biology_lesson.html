What is a Cell?

A cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all living organisms.

🧬 CELL
Basic Unit of Life

Key characteristics:

  • Surrounded by a cell membrane
  • Contains genetic material
  • Carries out life processes
  • Can reproduce

Types of Cells

Prokaryotic Cells

  • No nucleus
  • Simple structure
  • Bacteria and archaea
  • Usually smaller

Eukaryotic Cells

  • Have nucleus
  • Complex structure
  • Plants, animals, fungi, protists
  • Usually larger

Animal Cell Structure

🦠 ANIMAL CELL
Nucleus • Mitochondria • Ribosomes
Endoplasmic Reticulum • Golgi Apparatus

Key organelles and their functions:

  • Nucleus: Contains DNA, controls cell activities
  • Mitochondria: Powerhouse, produces energy
  • Ribosomes: Protein synthesis

Plant Cell Structure

🌱 PLANT CELL
Cell Wall • Chloroplasts • Large Vacuole
Same organelles as animal cells

Additional plant cell features:

  • Cell Wall: Structural support and protection
  • Chloroplasts: Photosynthesis, contain chlorophyll
  • Large Vacuole: Storage and maintaining turgor pressure

Cell Membrane

The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell.

🧫 CELL MEMBRANE
Selectively Permeable
Phospholipid Bilayer

Functions:

  • Protects the cell
  • Controls movement of substances
  • Maintains cell shape
  • Facilitates communication

Cell Division - Mitosis

Mitosis is the process of cell division for growth and repair.

Stages of Mitosis:

  1. Prophase: Chromosomes condense
  2. Metaphase: Chromosomes line up
  3. Anaphase: Chromosomes separate
  4. Telophase: New nuclei form

Result: Two identical daughter cells

Cell Division - Meiosis

Meiosis produces gametes for sexual reproduction.

Key Features:

  • Two divisions
  • Four daughter cells
  • Half the chromosome number
  • Genetic variation

Important for sexual reproduction and genetic diversity

Cell Specialization

Cells specialize to perform specific functions in multicellular organisms.

Examples:

  • Red blood cells: Carry oxygen
  • Nerve cells: Transmit signals
  • Muscle cells: Enable movement
  • Skin cells: Protection

Laboratory Techniques

Common methods for studying cells:

  • 🔬 Microscopy - Light and electron microscopes
  • 🧪 Cell culture - Growing cells in laboratory
  • 🧬 Staining techniques - Visualizing cell structures
  • 📊 Cell counting - Determining cell numbers

Safety: Always follow laboratory safety protocols

Real-World Applications

Cell biology is crucial for:

  • 🏥 Medical research and treatments
  • 🧬 Genetic engineering
  • 🦠 Disease understanding and prevention
  • 🌱 Agricultural improvements
  • 💊 Drug development
  • 🔬 Forensic science
