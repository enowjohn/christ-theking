⚙️ Metalwork Techniques

Metalwork Lesson for GCE O/L Students

Teacher: Mr. Peter Jones | Duration: 51:15

Lesson Objectives

  • Learn metalwork safety
  • Master basic tools
  • Understand metal types
  • Practice welding basics

Safety Equipment

• Safety goggles

• Heat-resistant gloves

• Welding helmet

• Fire extinguisher

Basic Tools

Hammer - Shaping metal

Wrench - Tightening bolts

Pliers - Bending wire

Files - Smoothing edges

Metal Types

Steel - Strong, durable

Aluminum - Lightweight

Copper - Conductive

Brass - Decorative

Basic Welding

1. Clean metal surfaces

2. Set up welding equipment

3. Practice basic joints

4. Safety check completed
