What is Algebra?

Algebra is a branch of mathematics that uses symbols and letters to represent numbers and quantities in formulas and equations.

x + 5 = 10

In this equation, 'x' represents an unknown value that we need to find.

Solving Linear Equations

To solve linear equations, we need to isolate the variable on one side of the equation.

Example 1:

x + 5 = 10

Step 1: Subtract 5 from both sides

x = 10 - 5

Step 2: Simplify

x = 5

More Complex Examples

Example 2:

2x - 3 = 7

Step 1: Add 3 to both sides

2x = 7 + 3
2x = 10

Step 2: Divide both sides by 2

x = 10 ÷ 2
x = 5

Practice Problems

Try these:

Real-World Applications

Algebra is used in many real-world situations:

  • Calculating costs and budgets
  • Solving engineering problems
  • Scientific calculations
  • Business and finance

Example:

If you buy 3 notebooks and each costs $x, and the total is $15, how much does each notebook cost?

3x = 15
x = 15 ÷ 3 = $5
