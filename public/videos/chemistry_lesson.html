What is a Chemical Reaction?

A chemical reaction is a process that leads to the transformation of one set of chemical substances to another.

Reactants → Products

During a chemical reaction:

  • Bonds between atoms are broken
  • New bonds are formed
  • New substances are created

Signs of Chemical Reactions

How can you tell if a chemical reaction is occurring?

Visual Indicators:

  • 🔥 Color change
  • 💨 Gas formation (bubbles)
  • 🌡️ Temperature change
  • 💡 Light emission
  • 🔊 Sound production
  • 🧪 Precipitate formation

Types of Chemical Reactions

1. Synthesis (Combination)

A + B → AB

Example: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

2. Decomposition

AB → A + B

Example: 2H₂O → 2H₂ + O₂

More Reaction Types

3. Single Replacement

A + BC → AC + B

Example: Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu

4. Double Replacement

AB + CD → AD + CB

Example: AgNO₃ + NaCl → AgCl + NaNO₃

Balancing Chemical Equations

Chemical equations must be balanced to follow the Law of Conservation of Mass.

Example: Balancing H₂ + O₂ → H₂O

Step 1: Count atoms on both sides

Left: H=2, O=2 | Right: H=2, O=1

Step 2: Balance oxygen first

H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

Step 3: Balance hydrogen

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O

Common Chemical Reactions

Combustion Reaction

CH₄ + 2O₂ → CO₂ + 2H₂O

Methane burning to produce carbon dioxide and water

Acid-Base Reaction

HCl + NaOH → NaCl + H₂O

Hydrochloric acid neutralizing sodium hydroxide

Laboratory Safety

When conducting chemical reactions:

  • 🥽 Always wear safety goggles
  • 🧤 Use gloves when handling chemicals
  • 👕 Wear lab coats
  • 🌡️ Work in a well-ventilated area
  • 🧪 Know the location of safety equipment
  • 📖 Read all instructions carefully

Real-World Applications

Chemical reactions are everywhere:

  • 🍳 Cooking and food preparation
  • 🚗 Fuel combustion in engines
  • 💊 Pharmaceutical production
  • 🧴 Manufacturing processes
  • 🌱 Photosynthesis in plants
  • ⚡ Battery operations

Practice Problems

Balance these equations:

1. Fe + O₂ → Fe₂O₃

2. Al + HCl → AlCl₃ + H₂

3. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂

Solutions:

1. 4Fe + 3O₂ → 2Fe₂O₃

2. 2Al + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂

3. CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂
